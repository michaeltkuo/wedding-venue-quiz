# Orlando Venue Quiz Question Review

This is a plain-language review copy of the current prototype questions and answer options.

## 1) Your Vision

Question:
What should your venue feel like the moment you arrive?

Answer options:
- Garden Romance
  - Lush grounds, flowers, open air, and golden-hour portraits.
- Old-World Character
  - Historic homes, arches, courtyards, and a sense of place.
- Modern + Editorial
  - Galleries, museums, clean lines, and city energy.
- Relaxed + Rustic
  - Wood, water, barns, natural texture, and an easygoing feel.
- Classic Elegance
  - Timeless rooms, traditional polish, and a dressed-up celebration.

## 2) Your Guest List

Question:
About how many people are you inviting?

Review note:
This is a hard-filter question.

Answer options:
- Up To 50
  - An intimate gathering, elopement, or small micro wedding.
- 51 To 100
  - A smaller celebration with room for your favorite people.
- 101 To 150
  - A mid-size wedding with a fuller dance floor and dinner service.
- 151 To 200
  - A larger day that needs meaningful room to move.
- 201 To 250
  - A substantial celebration that needs a genuinely large venue.
- 251 To 350
  - A large wedding that needs the biggest spaces in the current database.

## 3) Your Budget

Question:
What is your comfort zone for the venue rental?

Review note:
This is a hard-filter question.

Answer options:
- Under $2,000
  - I need a venue with an under-$2k option.
- Under $5,000
  - I am looking for a venue with an under-$5k option.
- I Am Flexible
  - Overall fit matters more than a fixed venue-rental ceiling.

## 4) Your Day

Question:
What kind of celebration are you planning at the venue?

Answer options:
- Ceremony + Reception
  - One venue for both parts of the day.
- Reception First
  - I mainly need a stylish place to celebrate afterward.
- I Am Open
  - I am still deciding how the day will be structured.

## 5) Your Setting

Question:
How do you want the space to work with the Florida weather?

Answer options:
- Outdoor First
  - The ceremony and portraits need to feel outside and connected to the landscape.
- Indoor First
  - A climate-controlled interior is the priority.
- The Best Of Both
  - I want outside beauty with a real indoor option for the event.
- I Am Open
  - The venue itself matters more than a fixed indoor or outdoor plan.

## 6) Your Area

Question:
Which part of Central Florida feels most convenient?

Answer options:
- Winter Park + Maitland
  - Historic neighborhoods, gardens, and polished estate character.
- Orlando Proper
  - Central access, city convenience, museums, and classic venues.
- North + Northwest
  - Lake Mary, Apopka, Mount Dora, Eustis, and nearby areas.
- East + Coastal
  - DeLand, Daytona, Port Orange, and the coastal side of the region.
- I Will Go Anywhere
  - I would rather follow the best fit than draw a tight map boundary.

## 7) Your Scale

Question:
Is a micro wedding part of the plan?

Answer options:
- Yes, Definitely
  - We are intentionally planning a small or micro celebration.
- No, We Want More Room
  - We do not need a venue optimized for an intimate event.
- Still Deciding
  - The overall venue fit matters more than a specific scale label.

## 8) Your Extras

Question:
Which extras would make the venue feel especially right?

Review note:
This is a multi-select question (select all that apply).

Answer options:
- Weather Backup
  - A strong indoor contingency matters to us.
- Overnight Stay
  - On-site rooms or accommodations would be useful.
- All-Inclusive Support
  - We would value built-in planning or catering support.
- Vendor Flexibility
  - We want freedom to bring in our own trusted team.
- Portrait Potential
  - A photogenic setting and great natural light matter a lot.

## 9) Your Planning Style

Question:
What kind of venue experience sounds most helpful?

Answer options:
- Guided + Supported
  - Packages, catering, and venue support would make planning easier.
- Flexible + Personal
  - We want room to choose our own vendors and build the day ourselves.
- The Fit Matters Most
  - I am open to either approach for the right place.

## 10) Your Priority

Question:
When you picture the right venue, what matters most?

Answer options:
- A Beautiful Setting
  - I want the space itself to do a lot of the visual storytelling.
- Guest Comfort
  - I want everyone to have room, good flow, and an easy experience.
- Something Distinctive
  - We want a venue that feels surprising, memorable, or unlike the usual options.
- The Best Value
  - I want the most meaningful fit for what we are spending.
